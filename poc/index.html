Load a Sample

Phase 1 proof of concept — pick a real local CSV (e.g. one of the files under data/samples/) to see which QC detectors have the columns and row types they need to run. This runs entirely in your browser (the File API) — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and no backend is involved. It mirrors, in simplified form, what src/data_loader.py and src/data_validator.py do on the real pipeline.
